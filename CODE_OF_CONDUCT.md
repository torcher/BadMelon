# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want BadMelon to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming project.

Participants are expected to communicate with patience, assume good intent where reasonable, and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
