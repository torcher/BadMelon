# Privacy

BadMelon stores information about families, recipes, pantry items, meal plans, and shopping lists. This data should be treated as private household data.

## Principles

- Collect only the data needed to provide the product experience.
- Avoid storing unnecessary sensitive information.
- Do not expose one family's data to another family.
- Avoid analytics that include recipe, pantry, meal plan, or shopping list contents unless explicitly approved.
- Keep secrets and production credentials out of source control.

## Account Data

The first release is expected to support direct email/password accounts and Google SSO. Authentication data should be handled through established platform libraries and providers.

## Future Requirements

Before a public launch, this document should be expanded into a user-facing privacy policy that covers data collection, retention, deletion, export, subprocessors, and contact information.
